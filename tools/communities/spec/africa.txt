# Conventions used throughout this file:
# - Jews of the Maghreb and Libya = mizrahi/jewish; Jews of the Spanish zone of Morocco (Tetouan, Tangier; Haketia speakers) = sephardi/jewish.
# - Europeans in colonies use the homeland key (french, spanish, italian, portuguese, english, german, afrikaner, walloon for French-speaking Belgians).
#   Naturalized Europeans of Spanish/Italian origin in Algeria still speaking Spanish at home are counted as spanish; the rest as french.
# - Creole peoples get their own keys: cape_verdean, santomean, mauritian_creole, seychellois, reunionese, krio, americo_liberian.
# - Regional keys (a 1920s source gives no finer split that fits in 10 entries): tanganyika_bantu, middle_belt, kwango_kwilu,
#   orientale_bantu, malagasy (= the "cotier" peoples other than Merina, Betsileo, Betsimisaraka), mandara (non-Muslim peoples of the
#   Mandara mountains, Logone and Mayo-Kebbi: Mafa, Mofu, Massa, Toupouri, Moundang...; the 1920s French category was "Kirdi").
# - Religion of African populations: Christian shares are mission-statistics estimates for c. 1925; the rest of a non-Muslim group is folk.
# - Shares of ethnic groups for colonies with no 1920s ethnic census are flagged E; where a 1920s census gives the race/nationality split
#   and the ethnic or religious split is inferred, M.
