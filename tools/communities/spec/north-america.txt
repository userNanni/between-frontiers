# Drafted from the censuses cited on each line and in the SOURCES block; `~` is the rest of the state.

# Region NAM — population composition on 1925-01-01 (Between Frontiers). Shares of state population.
# US method: race (Negro, Indian, Japanese, Chinese) = mean of 1920 and 1930 census counts over the 1920/1930 mean population (≈ mid-1925).
#   Immigrant communities = 1920 foreign-born white by mother tongue (first generation only; English-speaking descendants count in anglo_american);
#   exceptions keeping the second generation or old stock in the homeland culture: French Canadians in New England (foreign white stock, French parishes/schools),
#   Hispanos and Tejanos/Mexicans (Spanish-speaking regardless of generation), Cajuns, Pennsylvania Germans, Texas Germans/Czechs (estimates).
#   German immigrants split 65% protestant / 35% catholic (tradition). Hungarians -> catholic, Czechs -> catholic (incl. freethinkers), Greeks -> orthodox.
#   Catholic, Jewish and LDS (Utah church) shares = Religious Bodies 1926 members over the 1926 population (Catholic and Jewish counts in 1926 cover all baptized /
#   all Jews of the community, i.e. population, not adult communicants). anglo_american/catholic = Catholic share minus the Catholic immigrant/ethnic
#   communities listed (English-speaking Irish-, German-, Italian-, Polish-descended Catholics). Remainder = anglo_american/protestant (incl. unchurched).
#   Mexican/Hispanic: 1930 census "Mexican" (TX 683,681 = 11.7%, CA 368,013, AZ 114,173) and 1940 Spanish-mother-tongue white population (NM 41.7%, CO 8.3%,
#   AZ 20.4%, TX 11.5%, CA 6.0%) interpolated back to 1925 with the 1920 Spanish foreign white stock. In AZ/NM/TX Religious Bodies 1926 counts fewer
#   Catholics than the Mexican population, so anglo_american/catholic gets a 1% floor there.
#   hispano = Spanish-speaking old-stock New Mexicans and southern Coloradans ("Spanish-Americans"), separate from mexican (post-1900 immigrants and their
#   children): in 1925 Hispanos are US citizens since 1848, hold land and office, and New Mexico's 1912 constitution entrenches their language rights.
#   Folding: communities under 0.5% folded (Catholics into anglo_american/catholic, others into the remainder); groups with a relevance line kept down to
#   0.1%; with more than 10 entries the smallest are folded (relevance groups weighted x3), so some 0.5-1% immigrant groups are folded in the West.
# Canada method: 1921 census racial origin x religion by province (Canada Year Book 1922-23 tables 21 and 25) applied to 1925. "Greek Church" of 1921 is
#   not split: all Ukrainians are given as ukrainian/eastern_catholic (by 1931 ~1/3 were Greek Orthodox). Austrian-origin split ~half Ukrainian / half German;
#   prairie "Russian" origin mostly Germans from Russia (Doukhobors -> russian/orthodox); Dutch origin in MB/SK = Mennonites (german). Irish/Scottish/English
#   origin -> anglo_canadian (Catholic part = RC adherents minus French, Indian and immigrant Catholics). Indians -> native_american; Eskimo -> inuit.
#   Acadians counted as french_canadian (census "French"). Black Nova Scotians use african_american (descended largely from US Black Loyalists/Refugees).
