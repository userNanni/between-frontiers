# Region SAME: South Asia and Middle East, composition on 1925-01-01.
# Modelling choices for this region:
# - India: the Census of India 1921 language groups Western Hindi, Eastern Hindi, Bihari and Rajasthani
#   (incl. Gujari, Lambadi, Malvi) are merged into `hindustani`; Lahnda (Western Punjabi: Multani/Seraiki,
#   Pothohari, Hindko) and Dogri are merged into `punjabi`; Kutchi into `gujarati`; Western/Central Pahari
#   (Himachali, Garhwali, Kumaoni) form `pahari`; Nepali (Eastern Pahari, Khas) is `nepali`.
# - Indian states are modern units (IN-*, BD-*, PK-*), so 1921 district/division figures were reallocated to
#   them: flag M (census religion; culture by the 1921 language table and tradition).
# - Shia among Indian Muslims were not counted in 1921; shown only where the group is distinct and known
#   (Bohras/Khojas, Hyderabad, Baltistan/Gilgit, Kurram, Sind), else folded into sunni.
# - Tribal religion returned as "Animist/Tribal" in 1921 -> folk; the Hindu/tribal boundary was fluid.
# - Indian Christians take the language of their area; Europeans -> english; Eurasians -> anglo_indian.
# - Kuki, Lushai (Mizo) and Chin share `chin` (Census 1921 Kuki-Chin group); Iraqi/Syrian Turkmen and the
#   "Turks" of the Mosul Commission -> `turkish`; Turkmen of Persia/Afghanistan -> `turkmen`.
# - Arabs of Khuzestan speak Mesopotamian Arabic -> `mesopotamian`. Arabs of Transjordan -> `levantine`.
# - Maronites and Melkites both -> levantine/eastern_catholic (one community).
# - Folded without a key: Mandaeans (into mesopotamian), Yarsan/Ahl-e Haqq (into kurdish/shia), Baha'is
#   (into persian/shia), Zikris (into baluch/sunni), Kirat religion kept as folk.
# - GBR colonies: english/protestant listed where tiny but present, flagged with !GBR:C (colonial scope).
