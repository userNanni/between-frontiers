# Region EAS: East Asia, Southeast Asia (without Burma and the Philippines) and Oceania, 1925-01-01.
# Modelling choices (apply to every line below):
# - manchu: kept as its own key although most Manchus spoke Mandarin by 1925, because the Republic
#   still classified them as one of the "five races" (five-colour flag in use until 1928) and the
#   banner population was a distinct, recognised category; religion chinese_folk (shamanism and
#   Tibetan Buddhism survived only at the margins).
# - ryukyuan: Okinawans kept apart from japanese (Ryukyuan languages are not intelligible with
#   Japanese; separate prefectural regime until 1920); religion folk (indigenous noro/utaki cult),
#   not shinto.
# - Hoklo and Hakka in Taiwan and everywhere else = han. Overseas Chinese = han/chinese_folk.
# - mongol = all Mongolic peoples (Khalkha, Khorchin, Kharchin, Oirat, Buryat, Monguor/Tu); Daur,
#   Evenki and Oroqen of the northeast folded into mongol (closest listed community).
# - miao = Miao/Hmong umbrella (China census term); hmong of Indochina and Siam use the same key.
# - montagnard = Austroasiatic and Austronesian highland peoples of French Indochina (French "Moï",
#   Lao Theung/"Kha"): Bahnar, Jarai, Rhade, Sedang, Koho, Mnong, Khmu, Kuy etc.
# - Europeans of the Netherlands Indies (Dutch and Indo-Europeans, legally European) = dutch,
#   religion split 2/3 protestant 1/3 catholic as in the 1930 census.
# - Hadhrami Arabs of the Indies = yemeni (shared key; Hadhramawt is in Yemen).
# - Australian- and British-born whites of Australia = australian (English-speaking immigrants are
#   not split by birthplace); Irish-Australian Catholics = australian/catholic, flagged below.
# - Uninhabited dependencies get the owner's main community; the Spratlys get japanese because the
#   only people there in 1925 were guano workers of the Rasa Island Phosphate Co. (Itu Aba, 1918-29).
# - Christians in China folded unless >= 0.5 % of the province.
