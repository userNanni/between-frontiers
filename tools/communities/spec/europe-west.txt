# Western Europe (WEU), composition on 1925-01-01. Shares are fractions of the state population.
# Conventions used in this file:
# - British Isles: irish/scottish/welsh are national identities (birthplace), as the shared keys irish and scottish
#   cannot be language-based. welsh = natives of Wales; the 1921 census counted 37.1% Welsh speakers (aged 3+) in Wales.
#   Scottish Gaelic speakers (1921: 158,779 in Scotland, ~15-18% of the Highlands state) are folded into scottish.
#   Northern Ireland and Free State Protestants are irish/protestant (English-speaking, Irish-born).
# - Elsewhere language first: Alsatian and Lorraine Franconian dialects -> german; Occitan, Friulian, Sardinian, Low German,
#   Asturian/Leonese and Italian dialects folded into the national language (no census of the time separated them).
#   Silesian bilinguals (Wasserpolnisch) split half german, half polish.
# - "No religion" / dissidents (Berlin, Saxony, Hamburg, Netherlands) folded into the religion of tradition (protestant).
# - All Western European Jews -> ashkenazi (Italian and Alsatian Jews included); Gibraltar Jews -> sephardi.
# - Roma (Burgenland ~2.5%, Spain) not listed for lack of a shared key; folded into the majority community.
# - Colonial-ruler relevance (English in Malta, Gibraltar, Gilbert and Ellice) not flagged here: a !GBR line would hit the
#   English majority of Britain; colonial rulers are flagged with `!GBR:C` (colonies only) in the other regions.
