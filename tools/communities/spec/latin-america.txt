# Region LAM: Mexico, Central America, Caribbean, Guianas, South America, Falklands, Philippines.
# Composition on 1925-01-01, shares of state population. Format: tools/communities/generate.py.
#
# Modelling choices for this region:
# - National culture keys for Spanish-speaking republics: guatemalan, salvadoran, honduran,
#   nicaraguan, costa_rican, panamanian, dominican, colombian, venezuelan, ecuadorian, peruvian,
#   bolivian, chilean, argentine, uruguayan, paraguayan (mestizos, whites, Spanish-speaking
#   Amerindian and Afro descendants who have switched to Spanish). Indigenous peoples counted by
#   language, not by the census race question (Mexico: 1930 "habla lengua indígena").
# - Race kept as culture only for afro_brazilian (1940 "preto") and afro_cuban (1919 "negro");
#   pardos/mestizos/mulatos are in brazilian/cuban. Elsewhere in mainland Latin America Afro
#   descendants are in the national key (no legal boundary in 1925).
# - afro_caribbean = Afro-Caribbean people of the British/Dutch Windward/English-creole world
#   (Jamaica, Barbados, Trinidad, Leewards, Windwards, Bahamas, Belize Creoles, West Indian migrant
#   workers in Central America and Cuba, raizales of San Andrés). Dominica and St Lucia speak
#   Antillean French creole but are kept in afro_caribbean (catholic) as British West Indians.
# - antillean = Afro/mixed Creoles of Guadeloupe, Martinique and French Guiana.
# - Brazil and Paraguay Guarani: paraguayan speaks Guarani; one key. Brazilian indigenous
#   (excluded from the 1940 census) estimated as amazonian.
# - Aglipayans (Iglesia Filipina Independiente, 13.7 % of the Philippines in 1918) are counted as
#   catholic (schismatic Catholic rite; no better key). Moros = moro/sunni, pagans = igorot (Cordillera)
#   or lumad (all other non-Christian non-Muslim Philippine peoples, incl. Mangyan, Aeta, Palawan).
# - Mexican indigenous Catholics (folk-Catholic syncretism) are catholic; Huichol, gentile
#   Tarahumara and Lacandón folk.
# - Haitian Vodou and Afro-Cuban/Brazilian syncretic cults counted catholic (by tradition).
# - Mexico state shares: the 1930 census (población de 5 años y más que habla lengua indígena,
#   by entidad; national 16.0 %) applied to 1925; the per-state figures and the split among languages
#   could NOT be re-verified online in this pass (only the national 16.0 % was), hence flag E.
